# Brand icon sources and licenses

The provider, model-vendor and client logos in `brands.generated.ts` are vendored
as inline SVG from two permissively licensed icon sets. They are generated by a
script and never edited by hand: shapes and colors are exactly as published.

| Set | Version | License | Upstream |
|---|---|---|---|
| @lobehub/icons-static-svg (Lobe Icons) | 1.95.1 | MIT, Copyright (c) LobeHub | https://github.com/lobehub/lobe-icons |
| simple-icons | 16.32.0 | CC0-1.0 | https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons |

The only transformations are:

- internal `id` attributes (gradients, clip paths) get an `rlcd-<icon>-` prefix, so
  two icons on one page never collide;
- the single-path simple-icons get their published brand hex as fill, except curl,
  which uses `currentColor` because its dark hex disappears on a dark surface.

## Trademarks

All logos are trademarks of their respective owners. RLCD Gateway uses them only
nominatively: a logo appears next to the provider, model vendor or client name, to
identify where a request came from or where it went. No logo is styled as an
endorsement or implies a partnership. Unknown providers and clients get a neutral
monogram instead of a guessed logo. The browser globe and the RLCD mark are drawn
for this project (Apache-2.0, like the rest of the repository).
